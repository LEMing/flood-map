WebGPU solver spike — flood sim two-pass benchmark

Runs the virtual-pipes shallow-water solver three ways on identical input — CPU reference, WebGL2 (the shipping path), and an experimental WebGPU/TSL compute port — then reports per-step wall time and the max depth difference. Numerically identical output is the parity check; the WebGPU-vs-WebGL2 speedup is the decision data. Open in a WebGPU browser (Chrome/Edge/Firefox, or Safari 26+). If the WebGPU row errors, the message is the thing to relay.
